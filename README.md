# BANTHIC LABS

**Building useful things around AI, open source and the people who use them.**

Banthic Labs is an independent space for building, experimenting with and exploring software around artificial intelligence.

We believe the future of AI isn't just about bigger models. It's also about the tools, infrastructure and communities that make AI useful in the real world.

We build practical projects, explore new ideas and share what we learn along the way.

---

## What we're exploring

- **AI tooling** — Making AI easier to build with, integrate and use.
- **Agents & workflows** — Creating practical foundations for AI agents that can use tools and take action.
- **Open source** — Building reusable software that others can understand, adapt and extend.
- **AI & communities** — Exploring how AI can improve communication, knowledge sharing and collaboration.

Our interests evolve. These are areas we're exploring, not boundaries we're committing to.

## Projects

### Nestor

An AI assistant for Discord communities, designed to make community knowledge more accessible and help people get answers in context.

### Zetelton-Agent

A simple, reproducible foundation for building AI agents. Focused on keeping the essentials understandable without unnecessary complexity.

### Zetelton-Init

A command-line tool designed to simplify getting started with Zetelton-Agent.

## Our approach

**Keep it simple.**

Useful software doesn't need unnecessary complexity. We aim to build tools that are easy to understand, configure and extend.

**Stay open.**

We value open source, interoperability and the freedom to choose the tools that work for you.

**Build to learn.**

Experiments, prototypes and small projects are part of the process. Not every idea needs to become a product.

**Think beyond the model.**

Good AI systems depend on more than the model itself. Tools, interfaces, infrastructure and the people using them all matter.
